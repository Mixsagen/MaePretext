# MaePretext
A survival world that could exist without you

# Description:
It is a quiet vanilla+survival modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. It aims for the feel of old Minecraft: lonely, slow and rewarding. It is meant to be calm, not hectic. There are no quest books, no constant pop-ups, and no rush.


## The idea


The world is sparse and a little dead. You start with little, survive a demanding early game, explore ruins and caves, and bringing life back to the world


## What to expect


**Progression through Survival**
- Built around *Insane's Survival Overhaul*, with a harder early game than vanilla. For example, stone tools are gone, furnaces need copper, coal needs better tools.
- Ores are scarce and mostly found in caves.
- Healing is tied to eating, with steady regeneration while you are fed
- Dying costs you part of your items and XP, not everything.


**World**
- A reshaped overworld using the *Geophilic* and *Cascades* datapacks, with new terrain and biome features.
- Structures and dungeons from *Dungeons and Taverns*, *YUNG's* mods and others, spaced far apart so exploration feels like discovery.
- More End content.


**Atmosphere**
- Sound physics, footsteps and soft ambient sound. 
- Fog and darker caves and nights.




**Quality of life, kept light**
- *EMI* recipe viewer with recipe discovery, *Jade* tooltips, *Map Atlases* for hand-made maps, and a few small helpers.
- Performance mods (Sodium, ModernFix, FerriteCore, Lithium, EntityCulling and more).


## Requirements


- Java 21
- 6-8 GB of RAM allocated recommended
- A dedicated GPU is helpful.
